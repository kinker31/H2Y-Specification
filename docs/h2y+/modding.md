## HHX+: Heretic Extended Dehacked Plus
**BIG, MASSIVE TODO:** This could likely be replaced entirely with EXDEFS, once specification documents toll around? Nightdive really should get to releasing document on on that.

Actor functions, codepointers, and most other stuff I don't mention here can (most likely) follow MBF21 standards, anything that follows are features I haven't seen out of either MBF21 or EXDEFS.

### General Feature List Proposal
* Thing 1024 - 1077: Generalized Actor Slot
* Thing 1078 - 1085: Generalized Ambient Sound Slot
* Thing 1086 - 1093: Generalized Looping Sound Slot
* uint bPlagueSpeed: Black Plague Projectile Speed
* string TransIndex: Transparency Lump Lookup String
* uint maxAmount, keepBetween: Hold up to [maxAmount] inventory items, and keep [keeBetween] of them when exiting a level.

More to come as I see fit.
